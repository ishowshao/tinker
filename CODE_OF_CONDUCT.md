# Code of Conduct

Tinker contributors and community members are expected to communicate respectfully,
welcome constructive disagreement, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than
people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
me@ishowshao.com. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in proportion
to their context and impact.
